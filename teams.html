Our Teams

👶 U15 Team - Youth Development

Our U15 team is where young talent begins their journey at KR Academy. This group focuses on building the fundamental skills of football while emphasizing discipline, teamwork, and character development. Our coaching staff works to identify and nurture raw talent, teaching players the technical and tactical fundamentals they need to succeed at higher levels.

Focus Areas:

  • Ball control and dribbling techniques
  • Basic tactical formations and positions
  • Fitness and endurance development
  • Discipline and team ethic
  • Confidence building through competitive play

⚽ U21 Team - Elite Development

The U21 team represents the pinnacle of youth football at KR Academy. Players at this level have demonstrated exceptional skill and commitment. The team competes at a high competitive level and focuses on advanced tactical awareness, strength, speed, and the mental toughness required for professional football. This is our pathway to professional opportunities.

Focus Areas:

  • Advanced tactical and positional play
  • Professional-level fitness and conditioning
  • Mental toughness and pressure management
  • Competitive tournament participation
  • Professional development and pathway guidance

👩 Girls Team - Empowerment Through Football

Our Girls Team is dedicated to empowering young female athletes through world-class football training and mentorship. We believe in creating opportunities for girls to excel in sports, develop leadership skills, and build confidence. The team trains with the same intensity and professionalism as our boys' teams while fostering an inclusive, supportive environment.

Focus Areas:

  • Technical football excellence
  • Leadership development
  • Female athlete empowerment
  • Competitive match experience
  • Community building and support networks
